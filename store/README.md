Five primary English native screens per platform: home, appointment planning, required lessons, theory topics and settings. Platform originals are under screenshots/platforms/. Each supported watch model also has its own five-file folder under screenshots/models/.

There are 30 distinct platform screen files and 40 files organized by model. Images are native emulator captures, unedited and at their actual display resolution. No physical-watch photography is claimed.

For each platform's store asset collection, use its five platform originals. Current publishing guidance permits up to five screenshots per collection: https://developer.rebble.io/guides/appstore-publishing/publishing-an-app/
