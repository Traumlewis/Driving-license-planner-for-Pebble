# Driving Planner

**Developer:** Traumlewis  
**Version:** 3.8.55  
**Short description:** Plan driving lessons, get reminders and track your German licence progress.

Keep your driving lessons organized from your Pebble. Driving Planner brings practical lessons, theory appointments, reminders and training progress together on your wrist.

Plan up to 24 appointments, choose the lesson type, set the date and time, and decide when you want a reminder. Your next lesson is shown clearly on the home screen. Two reminders default to 60 and 30 minutes beforehand, with adjustable lead times and an optional second reminder. Vibration alerts work while the watch app is closed, and a ten-minute snooze gives you extra flexibility.

Designed for all 16 German driving-licence classes, the app remembers your selection and provides training profiles for relevant previous licences. Track country-road, motorway and night-driving requirements alongside your theory progress. Practical lesson types with a statutory minimum use 45-minute units with five-minute duration steps; extra minutes accumulate separately by class and driving type. Other practical lesson types are entered as whole units. Theory uses 90-minute units.

Check off theory topics or assign one to a theory appointment. Checkmarks count towards the corresponding theory total without double-counting an already completed topic. Manually added units remain separate, and checked topics are protected from manual subtraction.

After a lesson ends, confirm it to record the hours, ask again later or leave it for manual recording. Your progress increases only after confirmation. Hold Select under Required lessons to edit manual additions.

After the initial language download, lesson planning and progress management work offline on the watch. Optional phone settings make appointment entry convenient, refresh watch data automatically and offer Pebble Timeline integration. All 24 official EU interface languages are available on phone and watch. The language list and selected translations are downloaded from GitHub; an uncached language needs Internet access, Bluetooth and the open watch app. Only the selected language is stored on the watch. Training rules remain those of Germany.

The interface adapts to colour, monochrome and round screens, with vehicle icons, progress bars, theory checkboxes and navigation that remembers your selection.

**Supported watches**

- Pebble Time 2
- Pebble Core 2 Duo
- Pebble Round 2
- Pebble Time and Time Steel
- Pebble 2 and Pebble 2 SE
- Pebble Time Round

One PBW contains all six supported platforms. Touch controls are available on Time 2 and Round 2; all supported watches can be operated with buttons.
