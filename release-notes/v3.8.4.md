## Driving Planner 3.8.4

Restores progress bars for counters without a fixed legal minimum, as requested:

- **0 whole units:** empty bar.
- **1 or more whole units:** full bar.
- Extra minutes alone leave the bar empty until they make up a whole unit.
- Counters with a required minimum keep their proportional progress bars.
- Monochrome progress bars remain visible in selected rows.

The previous progress-row layout is restored. Round 2 and Time Round scrolling fixes from 3.8.3 remain included. Stored data and booking calculations are unchanged.

The combined PBW supports all six previously supported platforms. Install over the existing app to retain your data.
