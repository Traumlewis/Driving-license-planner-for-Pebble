## Driving Planner 3.8.3

- Progress counters with no fixed legal minimum (including AM country-road lessons) now show the recorded units and “No fixed minimum” instead of an empty percentage bar. Checked all 16 licence classes and 44 training profiles.
- Pebble Time Round and Pebble Round 2 keep submenu headings visible while scrolling. Extra space after the last item makes it readable within the circular display; returning to the first item restores its full view.
- Round 2 touch coordinates follow the fixed headings and respect the footer and hard scroll limits.

One PBW supports Pebble Time 2, Core 2 Duo, Round 2, Time/Time Steel, Pebble 2/2 SE and Time Round. Install over the existing app to keep your appointments and progress.

Native emulator checks cover all six platforms; both round displays were checked at menu boundaries in German and English. No physical-watch testing was performed.
